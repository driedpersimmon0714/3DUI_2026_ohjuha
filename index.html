Both gestures are available throughout the task. No stage switching.
Desktop: drag a lasso around the cubes, then click the floor to move the group. Select one cube and click its matching target to place it precisely. Quest: either hand — extend index finger with other fingers curled, draw a closed loop, then curl index to finish. RIGHT hand — point at cube, pinch thumb + index to grab, move, release to drop.

Both interaction techniques are available from the beginning. Lasso-select any number of cubes and move them as a group, or use hand ray + pinch to move one cube and release it on the matching color target.

Compact workspace, with the cubes starting in front of the user. For Quest 3S: use the LEFT index finger (other fingers curled) to draw a closed lasso; curl the index to finish. Use the RIGHT hand to point at a cube and pinch thumb + index to grab and move it. Controller ray remains a fallback.
